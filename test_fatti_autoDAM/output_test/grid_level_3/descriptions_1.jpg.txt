=== Conservative ===
The grass is a mix of short and medium-length blades, predominantly green with patches of brown. The grass is evenly distributed across the ground, with some areas appearing slightly more sparse. The blades are thin and delicate, creating a soft, natural texture.

=== Balanced ===
The grass is a mix of short and medium-length blades, predominantly green with patches of brown. The blades are thin and slightly uneven, with some areas appearing denser than others. The grass is spread across the ground, with some blades standing upright while others are bent or lying flat. The overall texture is soft and natural, with a few scattered dry patches.
